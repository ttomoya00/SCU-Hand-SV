# SCU-Hand with Integrated Single-Sheet Valve: A Funnel-Shaped Robotic Hand for Milligram-Scale Powder Handling

**ICRA2026** | [OMRON SINIC X](https://sinicx.com) | [@omron_sinicx](https://twitter.com/omron_sinicx)

![Concept](https://omron-sinicx.github.io/scu-hand-sv/concept.png)

## Abstract

Laboratory automation for solid-state materials discovery demands powder handling across diverse experimental conditions and a wide weighing range; to address this, we propose the SCU-Hand-SV, a soft robotic hand with a controllable valve, achieving dispensing within ±2 mg accuracy across a range of 20 mg to 3000 mg for multiple powder types through model-based feedback control.
